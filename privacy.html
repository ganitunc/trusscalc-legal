TrussCalc

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: the TrussCalc app collects no personal data. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no servers of my own.

1. Controller

The controller under the GDPR is the person named in the Imprint.

2. The app

  • Projects and settings: your drawings, projects and settings are stored on your device only. I do not receive them.
  • iCloud: if iCloud Drive is turned on on your device, the app keeps your projects in your own iCloud Drive (plus one flag in iCloud noting that the sample projects were added), so you can use them on your other devices. Apple processes this data as part of your iCloud account; I have no access to it. Apple’s Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies. You can turn iCloud off for the app in the iOS Settings at any time.
  • Purchases: the trial and TrussCalc Pro are handled by Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; I receive no payment details and no information about you. The app only checks on your device whether a purchase exists.
  • Crash reports: if you agreed in the iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives me anonymised crash and usage reports. You give and withdraw this consent with Apple, not in the app.
  • The app makes no other network connections.
  • No automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place (Art. 22 GDPR).

3. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, to deliver the pages and keep them secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, available website). According to GitHub, transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This website sets no cookies and embeds no third-party content.

4. Contact by e-mail

If you write to me, I process your e-mail address and your message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). I delete the message when the matter is settled and no legal retention period applies.

5. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Write to the address in the Imprint. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the authority of your country or German federal state.
