FAQ

Are my projects uploaded anywhere?
No. They stay on your device or, with iCloud Drive turned on, in your own iCloud Drive. I have no access to them.
How do I see my projects on iPhone and iPad?
Sign in with the same Apple ID on both devices and turn on iCloud Drive.
What does TrussCalc cost?
The app is free and every feature is open for 30 days. TrussCalc Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The trial never turns into a purchase by itself.
I have a new device. Is Pro gone?
No: Settings → TrussCalc Pro → “Restore purchases”.
Can I use the results for a real design?
No, see the disclaimer below.
