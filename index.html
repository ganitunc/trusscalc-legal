TrussCalc – Trusses and frames for civil engineering students

TrussCalc is an iPhone and iPad app for civil engineering students. Draw a 2D truss or frame on a grid, add supports and loads, and solve it with the direct stiffness method.

  • Trusses: member forces and support reactions
  • Frames and beams: N, V and M diagrams, deflections
  • Hinges, arches, point, distributed and moment loads, load cases G and Q
  • IPE sections to EN 10365, self-weight from the section
  • A4 solution report laid out like a hand calculation
  • English, German and Turkish; German textbook notation
  • Works offline; your projects stay on your device or in your own iCloud Drive

The app is free, with every feature open for 30 days. After that, a one-time purchase (“TrussCalc Pro”, no subscription) unlocks the solution report, load cases and self-weight, arches, live mode and picture sharing.

For learning only. TrussCalc is a study aid. Always check its results by hand or with other software. You use them at your own responsibility; they are no substitute for a structural design by a qualified engineer.
